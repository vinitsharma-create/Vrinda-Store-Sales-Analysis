# 📊 Vrinda Store Sales Analysis Dashboard

> An interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard built to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, and business trends for Vrinda Store.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Vrinda Store's sales data to uncover valuable business insights related to customers, sales channels, states, order status, and monthly performance. The dashboard enables stakeholders to make data-driven decisions through interactive visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

- Analyze sales performance
- Identify top-performing states
- Understand customer demographics
- Track monthly sales trends
- Evaluate order status
- Support business decision-making

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Conditional Formatting

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

- 📅 Month
- 🛒 Sales Channel
- 📦 Product Category

## 📊 Key Insights

- 👩 Women contribute **63%** of total sales.
- 👥 Adult women are the highest purchasing customer segment.
- 🌍 Maharashtra is the top-performing state, followed by Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh.
- 📦 **92%** of orders were successfully delivered.
- 📉 Sales gradually declined during the last quarter of the year.
- 🎛️ Interactive slicers allow dynamic analysis by Month, Channel, and Category.

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

- Target marketing campaigns toward adult female customers.
- Increase promotional activities in top-performing states.
- Launch festive offers to improve Q4 sales.
- Maintain the high delivery success rate while reducing cancellations and returns.
